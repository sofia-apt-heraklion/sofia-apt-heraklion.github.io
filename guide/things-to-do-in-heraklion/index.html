Things to do in Heraklion: a walking guide from our door

Heraklion is easy to explore on foot. Almost everything worth seeing in the city is 10 to 25 minutes from our apartments next to Georgiadis Park, and Knossos is a short drive away. Here is how we would spend a few days, with real walking times from our door.

By Sofia & Iasonas, your hosts at Sofia Apartments · Times are from our apartments at Athinas 26

Start with the Minoans

Heraklion Archaeological Museum 11 min walk

One of the most important museums in Greece and the best place to understand Minoan Crete: frescoes, the Phaistos Disc, gold jewellery and pottery from Knossos and other palaces. Go in the morning, before the tour groups, and give it at least two hours. Seeing the museum before Knossos makes the palace much easier to read.

The Palace of Knossos 7 min drive

The largest Bronze Age site on Crete, about 5 km south of the centre. It is about 7 minutes by car or taxi from us, and city buses also run there from the centre. Arrive at opening time or late in the afternoon to avoid the heat and the crowds, and bring water and a hat in summer.

Walk through the Venetian old town

A simple half-day loop: from us, walk through Georgiadis Park to Eleftherias Square, down 25th August Street to the harbour, and back through the market. The times below are from our door.

Lions Square and the Morosini Fountain 14 min walk

The heart of the old town, with the 17th-century Venetian fountain and its four stone lions. Around it you will find cafés serving bougatsa, the local cream pastry, for breakfast.

Venetian Loggia and St Mark’s Basilica 15 min walk

Two elegant Venetian buildings a few steps from Lions Square. The Loggia is now the city hall; St Mark’s hosts exhibitions.

St Titus church 17 min walk

A calm square and church just off 25th August Street, the pedestrian street that leads down to the harbour.

Koules fortress and the old harbour 24 min walk

The Venetian sea fortress guards the old harbour. Walk along the breakwater past it for the best view back over the city, especially at sunset. On the way you pass the arches of the Venetian shipyards 20 min walk.

Central market, 1866 Street 13 min walk

The market street for herbs, cheese, olive oil, raki and honey, and a good place to pick up edible souvenirs.

St Minas Cathedral 14 min walk

One of the largest churches in Greece, on a wide square with the small old church of St Minas next to it.

The Venetian walls and Kazantzakis’ tomb 13 min walk

The city walls still circle the old town. On the Martinengo bastion lies the tomb of Nikos Kazantzakis, author of Zorba the Greek, with his famous epitaph and a wide view over the city and the mountains.

Eat like a local

Start the day with bougatsa near Lions Square, try a Cretan taverna for dinner and order dakos, local cheeses, snails or lamb with stamnagathi greens. Our full list of favourite cafés, bakeries, tavernas and bars, with walking times and directions, is on the map on our home page.

With children

Natural History Museum of Crete 23 min walk

By the sea, with a hands-on area and an earthquake simulator that children love.

CRETAquarium 20 min drive

A large aquarium of Mediterranean sea life on the coast east of the city, good for a hot afternoon or a rainy day.

A day on the water

If you have a free day, we warmly recommend a sailing trip from Heraklion port to the island of Dia with Avanti Sailing: swimming in clear bays, lunch on board and an easy way to see the coast from the sea.
