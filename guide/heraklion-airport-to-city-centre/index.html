Heraklion airport to the city centre: taxi, bus and car

Heraklion airport is right at the edge of the city, so getting to the centre is quick. Here is everything our guests ask us before they arrive, from taxis to parking.

By Sofia & Iasonas, your hosts at Sofia Apartments · Times are from our apartments at Athinas 26

By taxi: the easiest way

Taxis wait outside arrivals. The ride to our apartments at Athinas 26 takes about 15 minutes and costs roughly €15–20; a night surcharge applies between 01:00 and 05:00. Give the driver the address Athinas 26, Heraklion (in Greek: Αθηνάς 26).

To book a taxi in advance, for example for your return flight, call Knossos Taxi on +30 2810 211212 or book on their website. You can schedule a ride for a specific day and time.

Ride apps

Uber and Bolt do not operate in Heraklion. The only app for calling a taxi here is FREENOW.

By city bus

City bus Line 1 runs from the airport to Eleftherias Square in the centre, about every 20–30 minutes, for about €1.50. From Eleftherias Square it is a short walk to us through Georgiadis Park. This is the cheapest option if you travel light.

From the port

Ferries arrive at the port just below the old town. A taxi to us takes about 10 minutes, and taxis wait outside the passenger terminal. On foot it is about 25–30 minutes, uphill through the centre.

By car and parking

If you rent a car at the airport, navigate to Athinas 26, Heraklion. The drive is about 15 minutes. There is free parking on the public streets around the building. It can be tight at busy times, so a smaller car helps. Please don’t park in the building’s private area.

When you arrive

Check-in is self check-in with a key lockbox from 15:00, so you can arrive whenever suits you; we send you all the details before your trip. The apartments are on the 1st floor, with an elevator. A pharmacy and a mini market are one minute from the door, and a supermarket is three minutes away.
