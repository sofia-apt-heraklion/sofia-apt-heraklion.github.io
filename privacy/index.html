Privacy

This notice explains what happens to your details when you visit this website or send us a booking request. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to answer you and host you.

Who we are

Sofia Apartments (Sofia 164 and Sofia 264), Athinas 26, Heraklion, Crete, Greece, run by Sofia & Iasonas. Contact: sofia.apt.heraklion@gmail.com.

Booking requests

When you send the booking form, we receive your name, email address, mobile number, dates, number of guests and your message. The form is delivered to our email inbox through our own Google account (Google Apps Script, which also sends you an automatic confirmation and keeps a list of requests in our Google Sheets); if that is unavailable, through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service. If you write to us by email, SMS or Viber instead, we receive what you send.

To catch typing mistakes in your email address, the form checks that the part after the @ (for example gmail.com) can receive email, using Google Public DNS. Only that part is checked; your full address is not sent.

We use these details only to answer your request, arrange your stay and meet our legal obligations as hosts (for example tax and accounting rules). We do not sell your details or use them for advertising. We keep them only as long as needed for these purposes or as the law requires.

Content from other services

To show this website, your browser also loads content from: OpenStreetMap (map tiles), cdnjs/Cloudflare (the map software), Open-Meteo (weather) and GitHub (the website itself and the availability calendar). Like any website, these services receive your device’s IP address when your browser contacts them. Links to Airbnb, Google Maps, Viber and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

On your device

We do not use cookies. The website only saves your language choice in your browser’s local storage, so that it opens in your language next time.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what details we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete them, by writing to sofia.apt.heraklion@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).
